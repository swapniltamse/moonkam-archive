## The images and videos: no copyright claimed by the contributor

The frames and videos in the release packages were made during NASA's GRAIL mission (2011 to 2012) by MoonKAM, a program run for NASA by Sally Ride Science with Caltech-JPL and MIT.

- The contributor claims no copyright in them. CC BY 4.0 does not apply to them, because they are not the contributor's to license.
- NASA mission material is generally not protected by copyright in the United States. See NASA's media usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
- Sally Ride Science was part of UC San Diego when MoonKAM ran. Confirmation that UC San Diego claims no rights in these frames has been requested and is not yet on record. This file will be updated when it is.
- Please credit: NASA / Caltech-JPL / MIT / Sally Ride Science.

Some award-tagged frames carried student names. They are not in this public release.
